## Texas-2k Line Rating Analysis

An agent parsed the Birchfield Texas-2k RAW file (PSS/E format) and compared to our ratings.

### Key Findings

**Our 138 kV is 2.4x overrated.** Texas-2k 161 kV (closest analog) has median rating 251 MVA.
Our 600 MVA implies 2-bundle Drake conductor, when single Drake (~250 MVA) is the standard.

**But our 138 kV network is ~2x sparser.** We have 1,267 constrained 138 kV lines; Texas-2k
has 3,292 sub-transmission lines. The overrating compensates for missing parallel paths.

**345 kV is correct.** Our 1200/2400 MVA matches 2-bundle Cardinal single/double-circuit
within 1%. The Texas-2k 500 kV median cluster is ~2020 MVA.

**Morgan Creek→Tonkawa at 1200 MVA is physically correct** — it's what falls out naturally
for single-circuit 345 kV with 2-bundle Cardinal conductor, not an artificial hold.

**Length dependence is weak (r ≈ -0.2).** Better explained by urban/rural distinction than
by physics (stability limits).

### Conclusion (CORRECTED late in session)

**The topology is NOT sparse.** The model has ~3,500 real 138 kV line segments total
(1,082 constrained + 2,431 unconstrained through SPL split points). This is comparable to
Texas-2k's 3,292. The problem is that we set 2,431 segments (70% of 138 kV network) to
999,999 MVA because they touch T-junction split points.

The SPL unconstrain was done because T-junction splitting creates artificial bottlenecks:
a single physical line rated at 600 MVA gets split into 3 segments at T-junctions, and
each segment gets independently rated at 600 MVA, but the middle segment can't carry the
original line's full flow. However, unconstraining ALL SPL-connected branches is too
aggressive — it removes realistic thermal limits from most of the 138 kV network.

**The fix:** Give SPL segments the same rating as their parent line (voltage-tier-based),
not 999,999 MVA. Combined with 250 MVA per-line ratings, this would produce a realistically
constrained 138 kV network with ~3,500 thermally-limited segments.

## Storage Integration

### Root Cause of Hang
289 individual BESS units create ~14k binary variables in Egret's RUC MILP. With constant
load, temporal symmetry lets the solver fix most binaries. With varying load, each hour is
unique → full combinatorial search → hangs indefinitely.

### Fix
`STORAGE_AGGREGATE=zone` aggregates 289 units into 4 zonal units (one per ERCOT zone),
reducing storage binaries from 13,872 to 192. Preserves total MW/MWh per zone.

### Egret Bugs Found
- `discharge_efficienty` typo in Egret params.py (silently uses default 1.0 instead of 0.96)
- SOC must be fraction [0,1], not MWh (PercentFraction domain)

- Jun 17 still sheds 317–1,004 MW at best configuration (actual: 0 MW)

### Strategic Direction
The next improvement is **restoring thermal limits on SPL segments** and reducing 138 kV
ratings to ~250 MVA. The topology is already complete (~3,500 segments); we just turned off
thermal limits on 70% of it. The SPL unconstrain was a blunt fix for T-junction bottleneck
artifacts — the right approach is to give SPL segments their parent line's voltage-tier rating.
