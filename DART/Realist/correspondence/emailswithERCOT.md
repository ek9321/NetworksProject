Hi Emmett, Please see a few quick thoughts below:

Calibration and validation without CEII: As Keith mentioned, direct model validation/calibration without CEII is challenging. Without full network topology and asset parameters, models typically cannot reproduce exact system operations. However, I think you can indirectly validate/calibrate by comparing with broader system behaviors—such as LMP patterns, congestion trends, generation mix, and other system-level metrics.

Applications for open grid datasets: One of the key benefits of realistic open datasets is enabling speed up the innovation cycles while supporting reproducible work without the need to go through long and complicated route for CEII-protected data.
 

For your awareness, we have also been working with Texas A&M as part of a 2026 initiative to make the synthetic grid datasets more representative of the ERCOT system.
